Live data. Fire Risk Rating comes directly from Washoe County's own GIS server (layer ID 96, "Fire Risk Rating," under Accela/AccelaWashoe) and covers only Washoe County addresses. Wildfire Hazard Potential is the U.S. Forest Service's national layer (2024 edition) — shown as a visual reference for the rest of the state, but not wired into the address search since its values aren't meant for exact point lookups. Hawk, Bug, and Stallion fire perimeters come from NIFC's WFIGS Year-to-Date service, the real official boundaries (slightly simplified for file size).
Address search: U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder (free, public). Fire perimeters: NIFC WFIGS Interagency Fire Perimeters (public, updated every 5 minutes). Fire Risk Rating: Washoe County GIS, layer 96 (Accela/AccelaWashoe).
